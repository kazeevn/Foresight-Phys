# Foresight-Phys Benchmark Analysis Report
**Date:** 2026-05-15

## 1. Introduction
This report evaluates the performance of the ChatGPT model family (`gpt-5.4-nano`, `gpt-5.4-mini`, `gpt-5.4`, and `gpt-5.5`) on the **Foresight-Phys** benchmark. The primary goals of this project are twofold:
1. Build a robust benchmark for assessing the forecasting ability and physical intuition of AI models.
2. Evaluate current state-of-the-art models on this benchmark to understand their capabilities.

The benchmark consists of 661 extracted quantitative fields across 26 academic papers, encompassing a variety of question types including boolean, categorical, integer, float, and formula predictions.

## 2. Model Performance Overview
A clear hierarchy in forecasting ability and physical intuition emerges among the evaluated models. Performance scales consistently with model tier.

| Model | Mean Quality | Mean Correct | Numeric CRPS | 1-Sigma Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **gpt-5.5** | 64.5% | 63.4% | 3.28 | 52.4% |
| **gpt-5.4** | 55.3% | 55.4% | 3.04* | 42.0% |
| **gpt-5.4-mini** | 50.6% | 51.0% | 5.64 | 35.6% |
| **gpt-5.4-nano** | 46.1% | 47.6% | 5.86 | 29.8% |

*\* Note: GPT-5.4 achieved slightly better overall CRPS (Continuous Ranked Probability Score) on numeric metrics compared to GPT-5.5 (3.04 vs 3.28), though GPT-5.5 dominates in all other quality and accuracy metrics.*

### Key Findings:
- **Scaling Law in Physical Intuition:** There is a monotonic improvement in overall mean quality and correct predictions from the nano version up to the flagship 5.5 model. GPT-5.5 successfully answers ~63.4% of the benchmark queries correctly.
- **Precision in Numeric Forecasting:** For numerical predictions, the ability to fall within 1 standard deviation (1-Sigma) of the correct answer improves dramatically from GPT-5.4-nano (29.8%) to GPT-5.5 (52.4%). By 3 standard deviations, GPT-5.5 captures nearly 89% of all answers, compared to just 60% for the nano model.
- **Categorical vs. Numeric:** All models perform exceptionally well on Boolean and Categorical predictions (ranging from 87.6% to 97.2% accuracy), indicating strong qualitative comprehension. However, the true differentiator between models lies in the harder numeric predictions (floats/integers).

## 3. Benchmark Properties & Difficulty Distribution
The Foresight-Phys benchmark succeeds in providing a balanced and challenging evaluation metric for AI models. The difficulty distribution across the 661 questions is remarkably well-distributed:

- **Trivial (all correct):** 143 (21.6%)
- **Easy (1 wrong):** 92 (13.9%)
- **Discriminative:** 144 (21.8%)
- **Hard (1 right):** 145 (21.9%)
- **Intractable (none correct):** 137 (20.7%)

### Question Type Breakdown:
- **Boolean and Categorical:** These questions are overwhelmingly categorized as "Trivial" or "Easy". For instance, 62 out of 73 boolean questions were answered correctly by all models.
- **Floating Point (Float):** Float questions make up the core challenge of the benchmark. They represent the vast majority of "Discriminative" (132), "Hard" (138), and "Intractable" (132) questions. This confirms that extracting precise, continuous physical quantities is an excellent test of deep physical intuition.

## 4. Conclusion
The Foresight-Phys benchmark serves as an effective and highly discriminative tool for evaluating AI physics reasoning. The difficulty is well-calibrated, with ~65% of the dataset providing meaningful signal (Discriminative, Hard, or Easy) to differentiate model tiers, while avoiding extreme saturation.

The ChatGPT model family demonstrates impressive baseline capabilities, with `gpt-5.5` showcasing a leap forward in producing accurate, well-calibrated numeric forecasts. Future improvements should target the "Intractable" float queries, which currently elude even the most advanced models.
